# CURSED EDGE

Mode: Experience. The arena and readable attack timing lead; the interface recedes.

- Existing NEON ARCADE cabinet is preserved. Only slot 3 and its internal game world change.
- Approved world: dark fantasy in a graveyard, burning chapel and ruined throne room. No robots or technological set dressing.
- Authored pixel silhouettes: red-cloaked swordsman, bone archer with bow, low wolf, floating ragged wraith, broad executioner, winged gargoyle, crowned king.
- Moss-grey stone and ivory bones in the graveyard; ochre wood and fire in the chapel; muted violet stone at the throne.
- Attacks are parryable. Normal melee enemies stop in front, show a white preparation cue and then strike. Indicators use pose, timing, sound and shape as well as colour.
- A custom pixel title is drawn in Canvas. Korean body copy uses readable system text. Controls use parchment/gold on charcoal with visible keyboard focus.
- PC and portrait/landscape touch use the same simulation. HUD remains at the edge. The paused/upgrade/ending screens stop combat.
- Particles are bounded; reduced motion removes screen shake. New assets are authored by the project; no third-party art or music is used.

One-handed play is primary. The character stays in the arena centre. The player aims and parries: touch drag direction + release, or mouse aim + click. A 130-degree guard arc makes facing readable. Sword attacks are automatic in the chosen direction. All enemy attacks, including the king's shockwave, can be parried; there are no unavoidable floor attacks. Arrow keys/WASD aim and Space parries as an alternate scheme.
